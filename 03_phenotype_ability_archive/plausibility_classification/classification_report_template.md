
## Required Statement

```text
This classification records the relationship between observed effect and biological mechanism.
It does not grant permission for deployment, transfer, or subject modification.
```

## Review Questions

```text
1. Is the effect directly observed, inferred, or reported?
2. Is there a known biological analogy?
3. Does the proposed mechanism violate organismal constraints?
4. Is the subject autonomous?
5. Is the effect transferable?
6. Is the carrier stable?
7. Is the subject a juvenile, clone-derived, converted, or command-restricted entity?
8. Does the record require Ethics & Risk Monitor escalation?
```
